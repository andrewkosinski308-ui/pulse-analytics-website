THE ENTERPRISE AI TRANSITION

Adoption Is Accelerating. Enterprise-Scale Value Is Harder.

McKinsey's 2025 global survey found that 88% of respondents reported regular AI use in at least one business function, yet only about one-third said their organizations had begun scaling AI across the enterprise. The gap matters: deploying tools is not the same as redesigning the workflows, governance, data, and operating model required to capture enterprise value.

88% Regular AI Use

AI use is becoming broadly established across business functions.

Scaling Remains the Challenge

Most organizations remain in experimentation or pilot stages rather than enterprise-wide deployment.

Workflow Redesign Matters

High-performing organizations are more likely to redesign workflows around AI rather than simply adding AI tools.

TECHNOLOGY & PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE

Architecture Should Reduce Complexity, Not Merely Accommodate It.

Enterprise technology environments may involve multiple websites, content systems, commerce platforms, applications, APIs, databases, advertising systems, analytics properties, and third-party services. The right architecture creates clear relationships between those components while preserving flexibility for future requirements.

Multi-Site Architecture Design systems that support multiple brands, locations, markets, or properties.
API & Systems Integration Connect platforms so information can move between the systems that depend on it.
CMS & Commerce Evaluate platforms according to operational requirements rather than forcing one stack.
Performance Architecture Account for infrastructure, assets, scripts, caching, and delivery as systems scale.

ENTERPRISE SEO

Search Visibility Becomes an Architecture Problem at Scale.

Enterprise SEO can involve thousands or millions of URLs, multiple domains, location pages, product catalogs, regional markets, JavaScript-rendered experiences, structured data, redirects, and complex internal linking. The objective is to make the organization's digital footprint understandable, crawlable, indexable, relevant, and measurable.

Information Architecture

Organize large content and product environments so people and search engines can navigate them.

Technical SEO

Address crawlability, indexation, rendering, canonicals, redirects, structured data, and performance.

Multi-Location & Regional

Build scalable search structures for organizations operating across locations or markets.

ENTERPRISE AI

AI Should Be Governed as a Business Capability, Not Added as a Gadget.

Enterprise AI introduces questions of data, workflow design, accountability, security, accuracy, governance, and change management. The most valuable use cases are not necessarily the most visible ones. They are the ones that improve a meaningful business process while keeping human responsibility clear.

Identify Find workflows where AI can create measurable value without compromising important controls.
Govern Define acceptable use, oversight, data handling, evaluation, and accountability.
Scale Move successful use cases from isolated experiments into repeatable operating processes.

GOVERNANCE & OPERATING DISCIPLINE

Scale Requires Standards.

Enterprise environments require clear ownership and repeatable processes. Publishing, analytics, brand standards, permissions, data handling, AI use, and technical changes should not depend entirely on institutional memory or one person's knowledge.

Permissions Roles and access aligned with responsibilities.
Data Governance Clear expectations for data ownership and use.
Content Governance Consistent publishing and brand standards.
Documentation Repeatable processes that survive personnel changes.
EXPERIENCE, ACCESSIBILITY & PERFORMANCE

Enterprise Standards Extend to Every Customer Touchpoint.

Large organizations cannot treat accessibility, performance, and customer experience as isolated page-level concerns. Shared components, templates, design systems, content standards, and technical architecture can help establish consistency across large digital environments.

Accessibility

Apply WCAG principles across reusable components, navigation, forms, content, and customer journeys.

Performance

Monitor loading, responsiveness, visual stability, infrastructure, assets, and third-party dependencies.

Design Systems

Create reusable patterns that support consistency across properties and teams.
