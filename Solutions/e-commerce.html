THE E-COMMERCE OPPORTUNITY

E-Commerce Is Now a Major Part of the Retail Economy

E-commerce is no longer a secondary sales channel for many businesses. The U.S. Census Bureau estimated $326.7 billion in retail e-commerce sales during the first quarter of 2026, representing 16.9% of total U.S. retail sales for the quarter. E-commerce sales were also up 9.8% from the first quarter of 2025.

That growth creates opportunity—but it also raises the standard. An online store has to compete for attention, communicate value, establish trust, make purchasing easy, and produce measurable business results.

CHECKOUT EXPERIENCE

A Customer Who Reaches Checkout Has Already Done the Hard Part

Getting a shopper to the cart is not the finish line. Checkout introduces its own usability challenges, and unnecessary friction can turn purchase intent into abandonment.

Baymard Institute's ongoing research places average documented cart abandonment at about 70%. Its research also finds that checkout design and flow frequently contribute directly to abandonment. That makes checkout a strategic part of conversion optimization—not simply a payment form.

70.19% Average documented cart abandonment rate
17% U.S. shoppers reporting abandonment from a checkout that was too long or complicated
23.48 Average default form elements in the U.S. checkout benchmark

OUR E-COMMERCE PROCESS

From Commerce Strategy to Measurable Growth

Every engagement is different, but our process generally follows a structured progression.

01 — Discover

Understand the business, products, customers, operations, market, competitors, and objectives.

02 — Evaluate

Assess the current platform, technology, store experience, marketing, SEO, analytics, and conversion performance.

03 — Strategize

04 — Build
