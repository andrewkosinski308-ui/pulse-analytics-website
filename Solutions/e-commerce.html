PLATFORM STRATEGY

Commerce Platforms Should Serve the Business Model

Shopify and WooCommerce are not the only options. Depending on the requirements of the business, we can evaluate other commerce platforms and architectures.

This may include BigCommerce, Wix, Squarespace, custom commerce environments, or more advanced architectures such as headless commerce.

The objective is to select technology based on requirements rather than selecting a platform simply because it is popular.

PLATFORM SELECTION

Choosing an E-Commerce Platform Is a Business Decision

Before recommending a platform, we consider the business model, products, customer experience, operational requirements, marketing strategy, budget, integrations, technical requirements, and anticipated growth.

A platform that works exceptionally well for one merchant may be the wrong choice for another.

Our role is to help determine what the business actually needs and then build the technology around those requirements.

OUR E-COMMERCE PROCESS

From Commerce Strategy to Measurable Growth

Every engagement is different, but our process generally follows a structured progression.

01 — Discover

Understand the business, products, customers, operations, market, competitors, and objectives.

02 — Evaluate

Assess the current platform, technology, store experience, marketing, SEO, analytics, and conversion performance.

03 — Strategize

Establish the appropriate platform, architecture, customer experience, marketing strategy, and measurement framework.

04 — Build

Develop or improve the storefront, products, collections, content, integrations, conversion paths, and supporting systems.

05 — Launch

Test the customer experience, technical systems, analytics, payments, navigation, forms, and purchase process before launch.

06 — Grow

Apply SEO, advertising, social media, content, conversion optimization, analytics, and other acquisition strategies.

07 — Optimize

Use customer behavior and performance data to continually improve the store and marketing system.
