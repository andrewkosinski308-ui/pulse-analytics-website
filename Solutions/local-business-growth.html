GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE

Your Google Business Profile Is a Local Customer Acquisition Asset

For local businesses, Google Business Profile is one of the most important components of the local search experience.

It can help customers discover a business, understand what it offers, determine where it is located, evaluate its reputation, interact with the business, and take action.

That makes the profile more than a directory listing. It is a critical part of the customer's path from local search to business interaction.
