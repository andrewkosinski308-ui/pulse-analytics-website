STRATEGY BEFORE POSTING

Social Media Marketing Is More Than Filling a Content Calendar

Effective social media marketing starts with understanding the audience, the platform, the business objective, and the role social media needs to play within the larger marketing strategy.

Different platforms attract different audiences and encourage different forms of communication. A strategy that works on LinkedIn should not simply be copied onto TikTok. A Facebook campaign should not automatically be treated like an Instagram campaign.

We develop platform-specific strategies designed around how each environment is actually used.

THE SOCIAL LANDSCAPE

Social Media Is a Market, Not Just a Feed

Social platforms have become major environments for discovery, communication, education, advertising, customer interaction, and brand building. Pew Research Center's 2025 survey found that 84% of U.S. adults use YouTube, 71% use Facebook, 50% use Instagram, and 37% use TikTok. Those figures demonstrate the scale of the opportunity, but the differences between platforms are just as important as their overall reach.

The strategic question is not simply whether a business should "be on social media." It is which audiences matter, where those audiences spend attention, what the business needs social media to accomplish, and how performance will be measured.

AUDIENCE INTELLIGENCE

Platform Choice Should Follow the Audience

Pew's 2025 research illustrates why a single social strategy rarely fits every business. Instagram usage, for example, is substantially higher among adults ages 18–29 than among adults 65 and older. TikTok shows a similarly large age difference, while YouTube and Facebook reach majorities across broad age groups.

Demographics do not make the decision by themselves. They provide evidence that can be combined with the business's customer profile, geography, offering, industry, purchase journey, content capabilities, and objectives.

SOCIAL MEDIA MEASUREMENT

Engagement Is a Signal. Business Performance Is the Outcome.

Likes, comments, shares, saves, and follower growth can tell us how audiences are responding to content. They are not automatically evidence of revenue. A professional social strategy connects platform activity to the stage of the customer journey it is intended to influence.

01 Reach Who saw it?
02 Engagement Who responded?
03 Traffic Who visited?
04 Leads Who raised their hand?
05 Conversions Who took action?
06 Revenue What created value?

The appropriate metric depends on the objective. Awareness campaigns may prioritize reach and attention; traffic campaigns may prioritize visits; lead-generation campaigns may prioritize qualified leads and cost per result. The important part is aligning measurement with the reason the campaign exists.
