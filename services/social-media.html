STRATEGY BEFORE POSTING

Social Media Marketing Is More Than Filling a Content Calendar

Effective social media marketing starts with understanding the audience, the platform, the business objective, and the role social media needs to play within the larger marketing strategy.

Different platforms attract different audiences and encourage different forms of communication. A strategy that works on LinkedIn should not simply be copied onto TikTok. A Facebook campaign should not automatically be treated like an Instagram campaign.

We develop platform-specific strategies designed around how each environment is actually used.

PLATFORM-SPECIFIC MARKETING

The Platform Matters

Your customers may use several social platforms, but they do not necessarily use them in the same way. Each platform has its own audience characteristics, content formats, discovery mechanisms, engagement behaviors, advertising tools, and strategic opportunities.

Pulse Analytics evaluates those differences when determining where your business should invest its time, content, and advertising budget.

STRATEGIC PLATFORM SELECTION

You Do Not Need to Be Everywhere

One of the biggest mistakes businesses make with social media is believing they need to maintain an active presence on every platform.

The right strategy depends on where your customers are, what your business sells, how your audience consumes content, what resources are available, and what you want social media to accomplish.

We help identify the platforms that make strategic sense rather than recommending social media simply because it is available.
