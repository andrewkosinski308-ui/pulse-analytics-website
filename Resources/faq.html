BEFORE YOU HIRE AN AGENCY

Questions You Should Be Comfortable Asking.

Choosing a marketing partner is a business decision. You should understand what is being done, how success will be evaluated, who owns your assets, and what happens when the strategy needs to change.

How will success be measured?Look for specific objectives and meaningful business outcomes rather than a collection of vanity metrics.
Who owns the accounts and data?Your website, advertising accounts, analytics properties, and business data should have clear ownership.
What happens when something isn't working?A credible partner should have a process for measuring, learning, communicating, and changing course.
What exactly am I paying for?The scope, deliverables, responsibilities, and assumptions should be understandable before work begins.
How are recommendations supported?Strategy should be connected to research, evidence, business context, and measurable objectives.
What happens if we stop working together?Understand what happens to your website, accounts, data, content, assets, and access before signing.
