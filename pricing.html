Pricing FAQ

Questions Before You Get Started.

Are SEO plans recurring?

Yes. SEO plans are ongoing monthly engagements because search optimization requires continued analysis, optimization, measurement, and refinement.

Are website prices one-time?

Yes. Website packages are project-based one-time prices, plus applicable fees. Ongoing maintenance, hosting management, and other recurring services are separate.

Is Google Ads included with SEO?

No. Google Ads management is a separate service so paid advertising spend and campaign management remain transparent and independently scalable.

How does AI pricing work?

AI marketing is sold as a managed service. Pulse Analytics manages the underlying model and infrastructure costs within the scope of the selected plan. Unusually high-volume workloads may require expanded scope.

Can I add services later?

Yes. The pricing structure is intentionally designed so businesses can add appropriate services and capabilities as their needs grow.

What if none of the packages fit?

Custom engagements are available for specialized, complex, enterprise, or unusual requirements.
